About

Assure is the workbench. getassureai.com is the public page. Updated 1 September 2026.

What it is

You type a vague question. Assure rewrites it into a precise prompt for Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, or another provider you connected. You can Copy the prompt, or Send and check the reply against files you uploaded.

What it is not

It is not a chatbot that invents a market, and not a hosted copy of your brief. This website is not the app. Compile, Send, and Verify on a client brief happen in the workbench on your machine.

How to start

Install from source, or join the waitlist for a packaged installer when one exists. Privacy and Terms are on this site.

Copy stays on this computer A Send goes only to the provider you chose
