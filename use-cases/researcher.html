How researchers validate AI-generated citations

A fluent literature review with a journal that does not exist is not a draft. It is a problem you have to catch.

  1. Pain

    Chatbots write confident reviews. They invent papers. Telling the model not to invent citations is a wish. Attaching a PDF to a web chat still sends that PDF to the vendor. Assure has no live search. If you need Google, this is the wrong tool.

  2. Solution

    Attach the abstract, notes, or PDF. Pick Research. Turn on Check against my files. After Send, publication names, dates, and percents that were not in the attach are stripped. Closed to the internet keeps the question on this computer when that is the rule.

  3. Outcome

    You get the claim, the method, and the limits, tied to what you uploaded. Missing evidence is labeled as not in context, not dressed up as a citation.
