# Product Hunt launch copy

Tagline: A trusted answer starts with the right question.

## Short description (260 characters)

Write a question. Assure rewrites it for the model you pick, checks the reply, and gives you one verified answer. Keys stay on your computer. A Send goes only to Gemini, DeepSeek, Claude, Kimi, or a runner closed to the internet.

## First comment / maker story

I was tired of pasting the same question into three chats and then arguing with myself about which reply to keep.

Assure runs on this computer. You pick who answers. Compare & Validate sends the question to more than one model and shows where they overlap. After Send, dates and percents that were not in your files get stripped. Thumbs on an answer change which format we try next. Those scores stay on this machine.

Copy never calls a provider. Send does. That is the trade you choose, not a claim that data never leaves.

Install from the repo, then `assure --web`. Open http://127.0.0.1:8765. Free is 10 Sends a day. Pro is $5 a month for 100.

## Differentiators

- Local-first compile. Keys and copy stay on this computer.
- You pick the provider. A Send goes only to the API you connected. There is no second Assure cloud.
- No vendor lock-in. Gemini, DeepSeek, Claude, Kimi, or closed to the internet.

## What it is not

- Not a new model.
- Not a prompt marketplace.
- Not a claim that Send stays on your machine.
