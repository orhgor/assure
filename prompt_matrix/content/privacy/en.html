Assure AI (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our platform and services.

1. What Data We Collect and Store

a. Data Stored in the Cloud (Server)

We store the following data on our secure servers (AWS EC2) to provide the service:

  • Account data: Email address, subscription tier, and Stripe customer ID.
  • Questions and prompts: The text you enter in the command bar or compile interface (stored in the database projects and run logs).
  • Uploaded source files: PDFs, images, JSON, YAML, and other documents you upload (stored in the substrate_vault table).
  • Compiled drafts: JDF ASTs, verification logs, and lock hashes (stored in the jdf_revisions table).
  • Audit logs: Activity and request logs for enterprise compliance (stored in user_activity_log and audit_log).
  • Waitlist submissions: Email, company name, and workflow type (if you join the waitlist).

We do not use your data to train our models. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

b. Data Stored Locally on Your Machine

The following data stays on your computer and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • API keys for connected providers (Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Kimi, etc.).
  • Workbench UI state and preferences (e.g., layout settings, theme preferences).

c. Third-Party AI Providers (When You “Send”)

When you choose to Send a prompt to a connected provider:

  • Your question and any attached files are transmitted directly from your session to the provider you selected.
  • The provider’s own terms and privacy policies apply. You must review and accept them before use.
  • Assure does not independently log or retain raw third-party API responses beyond what is persistently saved to your project files and drafts as part of your standard workspace history.

d. “Closed to the Internet” Mode

When “Closed to the internet” is selected:

  • Your data is not sent to any external provider.
  • Processing (including Z3 verification and groundrails) runs on Assure’s secure servers—not on your local machine.
  • Your data is still stored on Assure’s servers as described in Section 1a.
