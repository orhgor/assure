How Assure keeps the evidence.

What is recorded, how review works, and how data is handled.

Evidence

  • Every value links to its source page.
  • Originals are fingerprinted and kept as received.
  • Corrections are logged as new entries. They never overwrite the source.

Checks

  • Rules are explicit expressions, evaluated by the Z3 solver.
  • Severities are info, warning and blocking. A blocking finding sends the record to review.
  • Unchecked values are shown as unchecked. Verification is a filter, not a guarantee.

Review and audit trail

  • Reviewers accept, correct or dispute each item. A dispute needs a reason.
  • Each entry logs the time, the old value, the new value and the reason. Signed-in reviewers are also recorded by name.

Data

  • Processed by Assure on AWS infrastructure, and by vetted subprocessors under contract.
  • Used only to provide the service. Not used to train models.
  • A subprocessor list is available to customers on request.

Assure does not give legal, financial or regulatory advice. Your team decides.
