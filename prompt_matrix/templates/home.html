How it works

  1. Ingest. Originals are fingerprinted and kept as received.
  2. Extract. Each value is linked to its source page.
  3. Check. The Z3 solver runs math and rules. Unchecked values are shown as unchecked.
  4. Compare. MirrorSure checks against your master.
  5. Review. Reviewers accept, correct or dispute. Every action is logged.
