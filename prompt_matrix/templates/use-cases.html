Where verification pays for itself.

Any process where a document must match its source or its master.

Contract and price list vs invoice

For finance, procurement and audit. MirrorSure checks each line against the contract terms, and ParSure checks the invoice math. Each finding cites the clause and the line.

Insurance and medical claims

For claims operations and audit at insurers and carriers. Each figure is linked to its page. Totals and dates are checked, and claim items are compared with the policy terms your team defines.

Aviation maintenance vs specification

For maintenance quality and records teams. Values out of tolerance, missing steps and contradicting entries are flagged against the manufacturer's spec. This supports records review. It does not replace required sign-offs. The same approach applies to manufacturing quality.

Regulatory and plan audits

For compliance, audit and project control. Reports, filings or change orders are compared with the requirements or the approved plan your team sets as the master.
