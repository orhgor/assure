MirrorSure: compare and catch drift.

StatusMeaning
AlignedMatches the master
DriftDiffers from the master
MissingRequired by the master, absent in the target
ConflictSources disagree
  • Your master: your team defines or confirms it. Assure does not invent one.
  • Data, not redlines: values and terms are compared field by field.
  • Evidence on both sides: every status cites the master and the target.
  • Blocking findings: the record stays in review until they are resolved.

Examples: contract vs invoice · policy vs claim · maintenance spec vs card · specification vs test record · approved plan vs change order
