ParSure: extract and prove.

Fields from claims, medical forms and invoices, linked to their source page, checked and reviewed.

Request a demo
  • Source page on every field
  • Line items: charges, quantities and totals
  • Math checks: totals, tax and line sums, by the Z3 solver
  • Rule checks: dates, required fields and ranges, each with a severity of info, warning or blocking
  • Review: accept, correct or dispute. The original value is kept, and a dispute needs a reason.
  • One verified record: fields, check results and decisions together

Example: A claim's charges don't add up to its total. ParSure raises a blocking finding that shows the calculated and the stated amount. The reviewer resolves it, and the record keeps the history.
