Common questions

Do I need my own API keys?
Yes. Assure is BYOK. You paste keys into Connect on your machine. Your usage is billed directly by the provider. Assure charges for the workbench, not the models.
What does Pro include?
Pro is $5 a month for the workbench: 100 Sends per day, Compare & Validate, full history, and export (Markdown, HTML, PDF, Prompty). It does not include API usage. You still pay Gemini, DeepSeek, Claude, or Kimi for tokens.
Is Assure open-source?
The engine (PEM) and pem CLI are open-source. Install from source for the free CLI. Assure is the desktop workbench — a paid product built on those foundations.
