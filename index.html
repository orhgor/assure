The prompt matters as much as the answer

Assure is not a chatbot. You type a vague ask. It writes a prompt in the dialect of the AI you picked. You can Copy that prompt into any tool you already pay for. Send is optional, and the reply is a first pass, not the last word.

You type

Is this paper any good?

Assure writes

Research. Give the claim, the method, and the limits. Citations only from the attached file. Split Verified from Context and Logical Inference. Do not invent publication names.

Illustration of the shape. A live compile also wraps the dialect for Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, Kimi, or a runner closed to the internet.

FAQ

Is Assure just another AI chatbot?
No. Assure writes the prompt for you. You do not need to know prompt engineering. It restructures your question for each AI, checks the reply against your files when you Send, and gives you one verified result. Copy keeps the compiled prompt here so you can paste it into a tool you already use. The reply is a starting point for the next ask, not the endpoint.
Do I have to Send?
No. Copy keeps the compiled prompt on this computer. Send calls only the provider you connected. Keys stay here.
