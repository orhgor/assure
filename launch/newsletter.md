# Newsletter

Subject: Introducing Assure, AI you can check

Preview: Keys stay on your computer. You pick who answers.

Hi,

Assure is a workbench on your machine. You write a question. It rewrites that question for the model you pick, can send it to more than one model, and gives you one checked answer.

I built it because I was pasting the same brief into three chats and still shipping invented citations.

What it does:

- Keys live in `.env` on this computer. Assure does not store them.
- Copy keeps the compiled prompt here. Send goes only to the provider you connected.
- Compare & Validate shows where models agree and where they fight.
- Check against my files drops dates, percents, and publication names that were not in the upload.

What it does not do:

- Live web search.
- Shared team workspaces.
- Replace your Gemini or Claude subscription. You still connect those keys.

Install from the repo, run `assure --web`. Your browser should open. First run: paste a provider key. There is no password on this computer unless you set one.

Free: 10 Sends a day.
Pro: $5 a month, 100 Sends, Compare & Validate, full history.

If you write client memos, papers, or comparisons, click an example on Compose and see the overlap view.
