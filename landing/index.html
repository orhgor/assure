FAQ

      

        

          
Do I need my own API keys?

          

            Yes. Assure is a BYOK (bring your own key) workbench.
            You paste Gemini, DeepSeek, Claude, or Kimi keys into Connect on your machine.
            Your usage is billed directly by the provider. Assure charges for the workbench, not the models.
            On the Free tier you still pay the provider for tokens used.
          

        

        

          
Is Assure open-source?

          

            The engine (PEM) and the pem CLI are open-source.
            Install from source with ./scripts/install.sh.
            Assure is the desktop workbench — prompt assistance, validation, red-hat critique, and export in one app.
            The workbench is a paid product. The engine is open-source.
          

        

        

          
How is Assure different from open-source prompt tools?

          

            OSS tools give you the engine. Assure gives you the workbench.
            You do not learn prompt engineering, manage keys across tabs, or write your own validation logic.
            Compare & Validate, Check against my files, Refine & Verify, history, and export live in one place on this computer.
          

        

        

          
Is the preview on this page the app?

          

            No. The preview translates a dialect prompt in your browser. It does not Send.
            The workbench on your machine attaches files, grounding, and Verify.
            A Send goes only to the provider you connected.
          

        

        

          
What does grounded in your files mean?

          

            We check every answer against the files you upload. If a date, percentage, or publication name does not appear in those files, we flag it and replace it with "Data not available in this context."
            We do not claim to know the truth about the world. We stop the model from inventing facts about your data.
          

        

        

          
Is Assure just another AI chatbot?

          

            No. Assure writes the prompt for you. You do not need to know prompt engineering.
            It restructures your question for each AI, checks the reply against your files when you Send, and gives you one verified result.
            Copy keeps the compiled prompt here so you can paste it into a tool you already use.
            The reply is a starting point for the next ask, not the endpoint.
          

        

        

          
Do I pick Quick, Validated, or Refined first?

          

            No. Compare & Validate runs by default. Type a question and Get my answer.
            Advanced options can change the workflow later.
          

        

        

          
Do I have to Send?

          

            No. Copy keeps the compiled prompt on this computer. Send calls only the provider you connected.
            Keys stay here.
