# Product Hunt launch copy

ICP: privacy-conscious consultants, researchers, and analysts. Canonical draft also lives in `launch/product-hunt.md`.

Tagline: A trusted answer starts with the right question.

## Short description (260 characters)

Write a question. Assure rewrites it for the model you pick, checks the reply against your files, and gives you one verified answer. Keys stay on your computer. A Send goes only to the provider you chose.

## First comment / maker story

I got tired of pasting a client brief into three chats and then arguing with myself about which reply to keep.

I also got tired of invented citations. A fluent paragraph with a journal that does not exist is not a draft. It is a trap.

Assure runs on this computer. You pick who answers. Compare & Validate sends the question to more than one model and shows where they overlap. After Send, dates and percents that were not in your files get stripped. Thumbs on an answer change which format we try next. Those scores stay on this machine.

Copy never calls a provider. Send does. That is the trade you choose, not a claim that data never leaves.

If you write memos, papers, or comparisons and you cannot leak the source, this is for you.

Install from the repo, then `assure --web`. Your browser should open. First run: paste a provider key. Free is 10 Sends a day. Pro is $5 a month for 100.

## Differentiators

- Local-first compile. Keys and copy stay on this computer.
- You pick the provider. A Send goes only to the API you connected. There is no second Assure cloud.
- No vendor lock-in. Gemini, DeepSeek, Claude, Kimi, or closed to the internet.

## What it is not

- Not a new model.
- Not a prompt marketplace.
- Not PromptLayer.
- Not shared team workspaces.
- Not a claim that Send stays on your machine.
