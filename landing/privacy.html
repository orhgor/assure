Privacy

How Assure handles keys, questions, files, and the waitlist. Updated 1 September 2026. This is how the product works, not a sales claim that data never leaves your machine.

The workbench stays on this computer

The Assure app runs on your machine. Questions, uploaded files, previous work, and API keys stay there. Copy keeps the compiled prompt here and does not call a provider.

A Send goes to the provider you chose

Open to the internet, a Send goes only to the API you connected (Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, Kimi, or another you set). Closed to the internet keeps the question here. Assure does not receive a copy of the text you Send.

This website

getassureai.com is the public page, not the app. The compiler preview on the home page runs in your browser. It can keep recent compiles in that browser's storage. It does not Send. It does not upload your files.

Analytics on this website

This website uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-54F5NE9Y0P) to count visits and see which pages people read. It sets Google cookies in your browser and sends Google your page URL, referrer, approximate location from your IP address, and device and browser details. That is standard web analytics, and it applies to this website only.

The app is separate. Analytics is not in the workbench. Your questions, files, previous work, and API keys are never sent to Google. Nothing you type into the compiler preview on the home page is sent to Google either. If you would rather not be counted, any browser-level tracker blocker or Google's own opt-out add-on will stop it.

Waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we store your name and email so we can tell you when an installer exists. That is not your questions, files, or keys.

Sign-in

If you sign in later, the cloud stores email and subscription tier. Nothing else from Compose is stored there.

What we do not claim

We do not say your data never leaves your machine. A Send leaves, to the provider you picked. We do not say this website is tracker-free — it runs Google Analytics, described above. We do not invent accuracy rates or traffic stats.
