Common questions

Do I need my own API keys?
Yes. Assure is BYOK. You paste keys into Connect on your machine. Your usage is billed directly by the provider. Assure licensing covers the platform, not the models.
What does Enterprise include?
A dedicated sandbox for your team, audit packages for regulated work, and your keys or managed routers. Pricing is set per team — book a demo and we will scope it with you.
Is Assure open-source?
The engine (PEM) and pem CLI are open-source. Install from source for the free CLI. Assure is the desktop workbench — a commercial product built on those foundations.
