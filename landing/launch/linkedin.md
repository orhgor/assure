# LinkedIn

The AI tool that keeps your keys on your machine.

I built Assure for consultants, researchers, and analysts who already pay for a model and still cannot trust the paragraph it returns.

The workbench runs on your computer. You pick Gemini, DeepSeek, Claude, Kimi, or a runner closed to the internet. Compare & Validate sends one question to more than one model and shows agreement and disagreement. After Send, dates, percents, and publication names that were not in your files get stripped.

Copy never calls a provider. Send does, and only to the API you connected.

This is not a new model. It is not PromptLayer. It is not a shared workspace. Team edition here means unlimited Sends on this machine.

If your NDA, your reviewer, or your manager needs a source, start with the file on disk, then ask.
