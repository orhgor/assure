Six steps from idea to deliverable

Each step leaves a trail. Nothing ships on vibes alone.

  1. ✏️

    Draft

    You describe what you need. Assure reads your sources and proposes a structured first pass.

  2. ⚙️

    Compile

    The draft becomes sections, paragraphs, and tables, each with a stable place in the document.

  3. 🔒

    Lock

    Critical numbers freeze. Edit the narrative without silently changing the math.

  4. 🛡️

    Math Check

    Assure checks that locked numbers agree across the whole document, before you export.

  5. 🔍

    Stress Test

    An adversarial review hunts for logic gaps, weak claims, and missing evidence, pinned to the exact section.

  6. ✅

    Export

    You can't ship until Math Check passes and open Stress Test findings are resolved. What downloads is defensible.

Math Check: numbers that stay true

Assure treats your key metrics like promises. Revenue, margins, runway: once locked, they must stay consistent everywhere they appear.

Example

Locked: Q3 revenue $4.2M · Margin 32%
Math Check: ✅ Consistent across all sections

When everything adds up, you see Verified. When two sections disagree, Assure shows you exactly where, before your client or auditor does.

  • Locked numbers: one source of truth for every metric in the document.
  • Per-section status: pass or issue, without cluttering the Word export.
  • Export gate: Word and PDF only download when the document is clean, or you fix it first.
