Extraction and routing

Page quality, extraction confidence, and compliance-bound field flags decide when a field is trusted and when it is routed to mandatory human review.

[CONFIRM each signal below is shipped, not planned: resolution, blur, contrast, signature-mark detection, compliance-bound field flags. None are listed as shipped until confirmed.]

What is formalized and checked

Plausibility rules (for example, effective date before expiration, premium within liability limit), cross-document conflicts, and constraint solving for the figures and rules configured for the case. Missing or unverified required fields hold the document for review rather than passing silently. Verification is done by constraint solving over the configured rules, not by a second language model judging the first.
