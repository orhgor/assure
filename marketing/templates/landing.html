{{ strings.get('home.faq.5.q', 'What is Z3, and why does Assure use it?') }}
{{ strings.get('home.faq.5.a', 'Z3 is an SMT solver: software that checks whether a set of logical conditions can all hold at the same time. CrossSure gives it your rules and the facts from your documents as formal constraints. When they can\'t all be true together, Assure reports a contradiction and names the two sources in conflict.') }}
{{ strings.get('home.faq.6.q', 'How is a formal constraint check different from an AI model reviewing documents?') }}
{{ strings.get('home.faq.6.a', 'A language model reads documents and predicts an answer. A constraint check works through the logic: given these facts and these rules, either they\'re consistent or they aren\'t. Assure uses AI to read and extract facts, then uses Z3 to check them, and a person makes the final decision.') }}
{{ strings.get('home.faq.7.q', 'What does the Red-Hat stress-test check?') }}
{{ strings.get('home.faq.7.a', 'Red-Hat runs an adversarial pass on the draft before sign-off. It flags claims that aren\'t supported by approved facts, clauses that were omitted, and gaps against your policy. Each flag is a finding your reviewer approves or rejects.') }}
