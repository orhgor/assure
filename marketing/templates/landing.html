Four stages from raw case file to locked deliverable.

  1. 01

    ParSure

    Ingest and anchor. Parses PDFs, scans, tables, and ERP exports. Every extracted figure keeps its exact page, cell, or row coordinate.

  2. 02

    CrossSure

    Verify constraints. Compiles figures and domain rules into formal constraints across the case file. Every contradiction lists the two sources in conflict.

  3. 03

    Red-Hat

    Stress-test. Runs an adversarial pass against the draft to flag unsupported claims, omitted clauses, and policy gaps before sign-off.

  4. 04

    DraftSure

    Lock and export. Compiles the final Word or PDF document. Export unlocks only when all checks pass and open findings are resolved, with a complete audit record.
